Tech Notes for FOH

  • We are fully ampless. Guitars & bass run HX Stomp modelers — no backline, no cabinets to mic.
  • We bring our own mixer. You receive a single 7-channel XLR snake (channels at left). Ch 1–4 are mic level, Ch 5–7 are line level.
  • In-ear monitors — no stage wedges or monitor mixes needed from FOH. 0 MONITORS
  • Drums: please mic the kit for FOH at your discretion. Our internal drum "trash mic" stays in our IEMs and does not come to FOH.
  • Powered cabs behind Rob & Justin are fed from our mixer — power only, nothing for FOH to do.
  • Power: one standard wall/edison drop at each of the 4 positions, plus the 2 powered cabs.
